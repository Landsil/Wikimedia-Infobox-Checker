About this tool

Given an English Wikipedia category, lists the living people whose article has a missing, low-resolution or dated infobox photo — the gaps worth filling.

Given one person instead — a link to their article on any Wikipedia, or a Commons photo of them (file name or link) — lists every Commons photo that depicts them (depicts / P180), newest first (by date taken, else upload date). Each is marked good, low-res (under 2 MP) or old (taken over 12 months ago), with its author, resolution, dates, description and a Copy button; the article's current infobox photo is highlighted. Up to 1,000 photos are listed. A Commons category isn't a Photo Gaps input — use Infobox Finder or Infobox History for that.

How it works

  • Subcategory depth sets how deep the category tree is walked. Enumeration is chunked: Load more articles continues the same walk and appends.
  • For each person it reports whether a candidate photo already exists — via Wikidata image (P18) or Commons files that depict them (SDC P180). Old or low-res candidates are ignored. Each person has a Find depicting files button; Show Commons photos for all does every person at once (switch it off to go back; results are kept). The first 8 photos are checked — when none is good the card says how many Commons has, with see all N opening this tool's person mode for them (every photo, newest first).
  • A photo is low-res below 2 megapixels and old past 12 months.

Filters & export

  • Toggle which gap reasons show (Missing / Low-res / Old), filter by whether a candidate exists, and cap candidate age.
  • Export CSV downloads the currently-shown rows; the Sheets version uses =IMAGE()/=HYPERLINK() formulas so photos and links render on import (no sign-in).
  • Copy static URL builds a shareable link that prefills every setting and runs automatically.
