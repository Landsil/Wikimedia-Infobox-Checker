This browser

Self-test: reading, ordering, renaming, moving

Builds eight small JPEGs with real EXIF capture times in private storage, runs the full pipeline with stand-in names, and checks every result.



  
Face comparison

  
Pick several photos — two or more of the same person, and some of
  other people. Every pair is scored, so you can see whether the threshold of
  0.55 actually separates them on your own pictures. Nothing leaves
  the device.

  

  
  
  


  
Document roster (PDF or image)

  
Pick a programme, delegate list or screenshot that pairs
  photographs with names. Every page is rendered here, every face found, and the
  name printed with each face read if this browser has the model. Nothing leaves
  the device.

  

  
  
  
  

  


  
Badge reading (Gemini Nano)

  
Pick a photo of a badge, a name card or a screenshot showing
  someone's details. Nothing leaves the device.
