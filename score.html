Trial version. To score photos, this page saves a copy of Google's MediaPipe into your browser's storage: the vision runtime and three models — a full-range Face Detector (finds faces, small ones too), Face Landmarker (478 face points, eyes open / looking direction, head angles) and Pose Landmarker (lite) (body points, for framing) — about 23 MB, once. Everything then runs on your device; no photo or result is sent anywhere. Licence: Apache 2.0.

What counts as "good" (trial rules — every photo shows how it did on each)

    About this tool

    A trial: for one person, every Commons photo that depicts them, scored with simple, visible rules from face and body detection that runs in your browser.

    Input

    • A link to the person's article on any Wikipedia, or a Commons photo of them (file name or link) — the person is then whoever the photo depicts. The photos are those with a depicts (P180) statement for them — the same lookup as Photo Gaps' person mode — up to 300, newest first.

    What it measures

    In three steps. Every photo is assumed to show at least one face, so a measurement that can't be made is shown as ? (unknown) and not counted against the photo.

    1. Find faces with the full-range face detector (made for faces up to ~5 m away). Faces at confidence ≥ 0.8 are counted; the best one is kept even below that, marked as possibly small, turned or partly covered (dashed box).
    2. Read the main face from an enlarged crop around it — eyes and head angles — at confidence 0.8, or once more at 0.5 (shown).
    3. Body points for the framing, and a rough head direction (ears and nose) if step 2 couldn't read the face.
    • Faces and the main (largest) face's height as a share of the photo's height.
    • Head direction — how far the head is turned (left / right), tipped (up / down) and tilted, from the face model's 3D head pose.
    • Eyes — open or closed, and how far they look away from straight ahead (the model's eye-blink and eye-look scores, 0–1).
    • Framing — from the body points in frame: full body (ankles), three-quarter (knees), half body (hips), head & shoulders. When no body is found, a large face counts as head & shoulders.
    • Plus resolution and date, as on the other pages.

    The saved copy

    • Nothing is downloaded until you press Agree. The files come from this site (our unmodified copy of Google's MediaPipe), are checked against SHA-256 hashes, and are kept in your browser's Cache Storage.
    • On every visit the page compares your copy with the current one. If ours has been updated, yours is shown as out of date and scoring is off until you press Update. Remove deletes it.

    Limits

    • It detects faces and bodies; it does not recognise or identify anyone, and it doesn't judge sharpness, lighting or expression.
    • The face model is tuned for faces fairly close to the camera; small faces in wide shots can be missed. Photos are analysed at 960 px wide.
    • The rules are a first, transparent guess — they will change.
