Wikimedia

Small browser-based tools for finding and improving photos on Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Each tool makes read-only calls to the public Wikimedia APIs from your browser. (Infobox History can optionally save results to this project's GitHub repo — that needs a GitHub token you provide, kept only for the browser session.)

Infobox Finder →

Given a Commons category, find recently-uploaded photos that aren't used anywhere yet and depict a person, and compare each against that person's current Wikipedia infobox photo — so you can spot better replacements.

Photo Gaps →

Given a Wikipedia category, list the living people whose articles have a missing, low-resolution or dated infobox photo — and whether a candidate already exists on Wikidata or Commons. Or give one person's article or photo to list every Commons photo that depicts them, newest first.

Infobox History →

Given a photo or an article, show the current infobox photo of the depicted person plus the photos it replaced, side by side — with each photo's crop variants. Or give a Commons photo category to list everyone whose current infobox photo is from it, with their history.

Trial photo score →

Trial: for one person, score every Commons photo of them with face and body detection that runs in your browser — one face, eyes open, facing the camera, framing — using simple rules shown on the page.

Headshots →

Installable, offline tool for an event shoot: photograph each person's badge, then the person, and this reads the names, groups the headshots and renames them into a done folder. Runs entirely on your own device.
