Wikimedia tools

Small browser-based tools for finding and improving photos on Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Nothing is installed or stored; each tool makes read-only calls to the public Wikimedia APIs from your browser.

Infobox Finder →

Given a Commons category, find recently-uploaded photos that aren't used anywhere yet and depict a person, and compare each against that person's current Wikipedia infobox photo — so you can spot better replacements.

Photo Gaps →

Given a Wikipedia category, list the living people whose articles have a missing, low-resolution or dated infobox photo — and whether a candidate already exists on Wikidata or Commons.
